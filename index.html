Purpose of Google Account Integration and Data Usage

We use Google APIs solely to create, store, and restore backup files in the app-specific storage area (appDataFolder) of the user's own Google Drive. We do not access any other files in the user's Google Drive, nor do we share, provide, or sell user data to third parties.
